Color palettes

Palette colors are based on the combinations in A Dictionary of Color Combinations (配色総鑑 / Haishoku Soukan) by Sanzo Wada (和田三造, 1883–1967), published by Seigensha Art Publishing. ShiftLog is not affiliated with or endorsed by the author, his estate or the publisher. If you like these colors, please support the book.
Color values come from the open dataset dictionary-of-colour-combinations by Matt DesLauriers, originally compiled by Dain M. Blodorn Kim (sanzo-wada). Dark backgrounds were darkened and the light-mode versions derived by ShiftLog from these colors.
